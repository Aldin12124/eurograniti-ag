Tradition & Precision

Since our inception in Landovice-Prizren, EURO GRANITI-AG has been synonymous with quality and durability. Under the leadership of Rexhep Thaqi, we have transformed raw blocks of granite, marble, and modern laminam into architectural masterpieces.
